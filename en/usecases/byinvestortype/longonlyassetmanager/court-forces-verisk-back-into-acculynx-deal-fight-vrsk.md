## Relevant

This is a quality compounder where the hold decision now turns on a capital-allocation and portfolio-shape question, not the quarter.
## Compelling

The platform frames the overhang: a Delaware Chancery ruling forces Verisk to keep performing on the $2.35B AccuLynx deal it had tried to terminate, the stock fell about 5.6% to $181.11, and any close still needs FTC clearance.
## Workflow

The long-only PM gets a ready-made read on whether management recommits to or keeps contesting a deal that would push Verisk beyond its insurance-analytics core into contractor workflow software, a clear input to a multi-year hold.
