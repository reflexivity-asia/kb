## Relevant

A fatal Bab el-Mandeb attack revives Red Sea chokepoint risk, lifting energy, freight and insurance costs and pressuring oil-importing, trade-sensitive emerging markets, with EEM near $65.62 (+0.69%) as a liquid sentiment gauge.
## Compelling

The read-through runs through crude, not headline trade data: Maersk and Hapag-Lloyd are already rerouting, so a single strike becomes a broader trade-lane risk premium that hits inflation-sensitive assets first.
## Workflow

The advisor can send a short client note framing this as an oil-and-inflation watchpoint, positioning EEM as a broad risk barometer rather than a direct shipping trade, and flag energy exposure as the hedge.
