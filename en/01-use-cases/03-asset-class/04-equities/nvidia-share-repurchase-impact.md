> NVIDIA announced an additional $150 billion share-repurchase authorization. What is the likely impact, and how does the market reaction compare with other large buyback announcements?

## Scale of the authorization

The supplied analysis says NVIDIA's board approved an additional **$150.0B** on 2026-09-28, taking remaining authorization through FY2028 to **$235.0B**. It framed the increase against the company's scale:

| Source metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Additional authorization | $150.0B |
| Remaining authorization through FY2028 | $235.0B |
| Approx. market capitalization used in the analysis | ~$5.6T |
| Additional authorization / market cap | ~2.7% |
| FY2026 free cash flow | ~$96.7B |
| Additional authorization / FY2026 FCF | ~1.55x |
| FY2026 actual repurchases | $48.0B |

The source interpretation was that the authorization was large but financially supported by NVIDIA's cash generation.

## Compare the market reaction with earlier large buybacks

| Company | Announcement | Date | Next trading day | About one month later |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| NVIDIA | +$150B | 2026-09-28 | +1.7% | — |
| Meta | $50B | 2024-02-01 | +20.3% | +27.2% |
| Alphabet | $70B | 2024-04-25 | +10.2% | +12.2% |
| Apple | $110B | 2024-05-02 | +6.0% | +11.1% |

The source cautions that Meta, Alphabet and Apple announced their programs alongside earnings, so those price moves cannot be attributed to buybacks alone. NVIDIA's smaller next-day move was therefore interpreted in the context of a stand-alone authorization increase and already-strong expectations for cash generation.

## What the example demonstrates

The workflow combines company financial statements, price time series, catalyst insights and news to answer three connected questions: how large the authorization is relative to financial capacity, how the stock reacted, and whether historical analogues are genuinely comparable.

## Limitations

- An authorization is a ceiling, not a commitment to repurchase the full amount immediately.
- The historical comparison contains only three selected companies and is not a statistical generalization.
- The comparison-company reactions overlapped with earnings announcements.
- The market-cap estimate in the source was approximate.
