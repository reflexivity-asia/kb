# Earnings Insights

Reflexivity’s Earnings Insights, powered by the Knowledge Graph (KG), are driven by an AI-processing pipeline that aggregates and distills the most actionable information from company press releases, filings, transcripts, and financial news sources during earnings season. This product features two core insight types: Previews (generated 3d prior to release) and Recaps (generated after company reports). Insights are available in multiple languages.
