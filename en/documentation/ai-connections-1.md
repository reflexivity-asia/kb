# AI Connections

[← Documentation](README.md)
Connect Reflexivity to your AI application to search published research and explore company relationships. Ask questions in your application and use Reflexivity’s Insights and Knowledge Graph in the answer.

The connection is read-only. You can retrieve research and use your available saved watchlists and baskets, but you cannot change your Reflexivity account or lists through the connection.

## What you can research
Insights includes earnings recaps and previews, company and market catalysts, and scenario insights. Search by company, theme, research type, or date, then retrieve the available reports in full. You can also search across a saved Reflexivity watchlist or basket.

Knowledge Graph connects companies to industry, financial, and macro themes, products, countries, regions, and competitors. Explore a company’s exposures, find companies associated with a theme, and request the evidence behind a relationship.

Use them together to investigate a question: find companies associated with a theme, read their recent earnings research, and compare what they reported. Comparisons depend on the coverage and figures available in the research.

See [Research examples](ai-connections-1/research.md) for questions to try, or [Coverage and sources](ai-connections-1/research/coverage-and-sources.md) for help interpreting rankings, dates, evidence, and limitations.

## Research coverage
This connection provides Reflexivity Insights and Knowledge Graph research. It does not provide a general-purpose tool for retrieving live market quotes or historical price series.

Published research may contain prices, financial figures, or scenario forecasts. Those figures belong to that research and should be interpreted using its dates and context.

Other Reflexivity products and APIs may provide additional data. Their capabilities are separate from this AI connection.

## Get connected
You need your own full Reflexivity user account. If your organization manages your AI application, an administrator may need to make the integration available first.

Connecting for yourself? [Choose your application](ai-connections-1/connect.md), then follow its guide.

Setting up access for a team? Start with [Set up your organization](ai-connections-1/connect/set-up-your-organization.md).

After signing in, return to your application and complete its connection check. A successful browser sign-in does not by itself confirm that the application can call Reflexivity’s tools.
