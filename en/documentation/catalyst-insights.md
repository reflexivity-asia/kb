# Catalyst Insights

[← Documentation](README.md)
Reflexivity Catalysts explain why stocks, sectors, and themes move - connecting market behavior to the underlying business, thematic and macro drivers. Designed for hedge fund workflows, Catalyst Insights translate real-time market activity into structured, actionable context by identifying the triggering event, mapping second-order impacts, and surfacing related opportunities and risks.

Across every material market move, Catalyst Insights answer three core questions:

- What is moving? ​ Which companies or sectors are experiencing significant price movement
- Why is it happening? ​ The specific catalyst or narrative driving the movement
- Who else is impacted? ​ The related companies, themes, and sectors with direct or indirect exposure By combining price-based, volume-based, and story-based detection, Reflexivity captures Catalysts regardless of where they first emerge - market activity, trading behavior, or breaking news - ensuring comprehensive and timely coverage. Catalyst Insights are delivered across five daily intervals (pre-market, three intraday, post-market) and cover material events across the S&P1500.

## Insight Types

## Company Catalysts
Single company events that may have spillover effects across peers, customers, suppliers, or competitors:

- Corporate Actions & Strategic Announcements: ​ M&A, partnerships, divestitures, capital structure changes
- Management & Leadership Changes: ​ Executive appointments/departures, board changes, succession planning
- Financial & Operational Updates: ​ Guidance revisions, operational disruptions, product developments, customer wins/losses
- Legal & Regulatory Company Events: ​ Lawsuits, SEC investigations, compliance issues, IP developments
## Market Catalysts
Broad developments impacting multiple companies through shared exposures:

- Sector & Industry Themes: ​ Regulatory changes, technology disruption, competitive dynamics, supply chain events
- Macroeconomic & Policy Drivers: ​ Fed policy, fiscal policy, trade policy, economic data releases, currency events
- Geopolitical & Global Themes: ​ Trade relations, sanctions, conflicts, elections, international agreements
