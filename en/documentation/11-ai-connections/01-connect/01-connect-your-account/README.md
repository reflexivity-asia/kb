# Connect your account

Each person connects with their own full Reflexivity user account. You also need your chosen AI application and a browser for sign-in. If your organization manages the application, it may need to make Reflexivity available first.

---

## Connect in three steps

Step 1: [Choose your application](../README.md) and follow its installation guide.

Step 2: Sign in to Reflexivity when prompted. For the plugin routes, sign-in opens in your browser at identity.reflexivity.com. Complete sign-in, then return to your AI application.

Step 3: Complete the connection check in the guide, then try a [research example](../../03-research/README.md).

---

## Check that you are connected

A browser sign-in confirmation is only one step. Your application must also discover Reflexivity’s tools and successfully call them.

The plugin setup workflow checks tool availability and account access by reading your available watchlists and baskets. An empty list can be a valid result; it does not mean sign-in failed. A timeout, authentication error, or unavailable source is different from a successful empty result.

For an application without the plugin setup workflow, ask:

“Use Reflexivity to list my available watchlists and baskets. If you cannot call its tools, tell me that the connection check could not be completed.”

---

## Sign-in and account help

Sign in in the browser. Never paste your password or an access token into chat. Installing the plugin or receiving it from your administrator does not by itself establish your Reflexivity account’s access.

See [Troubleshooting](../../04-help/01-troubleshooting/README.md) for missing tools, failed calls, or reconnecting.

See [Access and privacy](../../04-help/02-access-and-privacy/README.md) for permissions and disconnecting.

For account assistance, contact [support@reflexivity.com](mailto:support@reflexivity.com).
