# Access and privacy

[Help](../README.md) · [Documentation](../../../README.md)

---

## Account access

Access is for full Reflexivity users. You need your own account and an application that can connect to it. Start with [Connect](../../connect/README.md).

An administrator making the connection available is separate from your account's permission to read Reflexivity research.

---

## What the application can do

The connection reads published research, company relationships, and information about watchlists and baskets available to your account. It can use a saved list to narrow a research question.

It cannot create or edit watchlists, change settings, or modify your Reflexivity data. The saved-list listing returns names, identifiers, and counts; research requested across a list can identify its companies.

---

## What is shared

Your questions and the research used to answer them are processed by your AI application and its provider under their terms. Requests sent to Reflexivity identify the companies, themes, research, date ranges, or saved lists needed for the question.

Your organization's AI-use policies may also apply.

---

## Sign-in

The plugin opens Reflexivity sign-in in your browser. Complete it there; do not paste passwords or sign-in codes into a chat. The application uses a sign-in token to request research on your behalf.

---

## Removing access

Disable or remove the Reflexivity connection in each AI application where you use it. If you installed a plugin, follow that application’s controls for disconnecting the account and removing the plugin.

Removing a connection from an application and revoking its authorization are separate actions. For help revoking authorization to your Reflexivity account, contact [support@reflexivity.com](mailto:support@reflexivity.com).

Disconnecting an application does not cancel your Reflexivity subscription.

---

## Using research

Check the underlying research, reporting periods, and available evidence before relying on an AI-generated answer.

See [Coverage and sources](../../research/coverage-and-sources/README.md) for help interpreting results.

Use of Reflexivity research is subject to your applicable agreement and the [Terms of Service](https://reflexivity.com/en/terms-of-service). For information about personal data, see the [Privacy Policy](https://reflexivity.com/en/privacy-policy).

Your AI application’s terms and your organization’s policies also apply.

For questions about access or permitted use, contact [support@reflexivity.com](mailto:support@reflexivity.com).
