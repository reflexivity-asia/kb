## Workflow

1. Start with the dashboard indicator to see the directional balance of market signals.
2. Open an individual market card for its composite score and contributing drivers.
3. Review the historical series for the same indicator.
4. Compare the indicator with the market's actual price or yield movement rather than treating the score in isolation.

The supplied screenshots demonstrate this workflow for both US and Japanese markets, including equity and government-bond views. The detailed panels show the composite indicator together with the underlying signal history, while the comparison views place the indicator beside the actual market series.

## Why this is useful

The example is not presented as a stand-alone forecast. Its practical value is the ability to move from a dashboard-level directional signal into the underlying detail and then check whether the signal has tracked the market move being studied.
