# Sysco beats and lifts the FY27 setup (SYY)

**Persona:** Long-only Asset Manager  
**Insight type:** Earnings Catalyst  
**Signal:** Bullish  
**Date:** August 4, 2026

> These examples are dated platform outputs. Check them against current market data before using them, or present them as illustrative examples only.

## Relevant

This is a quality compounder where the long-term hold decision turns on capital allocation and margin execution, not the headline beat.
## Compelling

The platform separates the print from the debate: Q4 adj EPS $1.53 and sales $22.1B beat, and the 53-week FY27 guide calls for 6-7% sales and 9-11% adjusted EPS growth, but GAAP EPS of $1.15 and a 17bps gross-margin dip show deal costs and mix still cloud the story.
## Workflow

The PM gets a ready-made read on capital-allocation posture ($2.1B free cash flow, 2.7x net leverage, the Jetro deal) to judge whether shareholder-return capacity holds up in a multi-year position review.
