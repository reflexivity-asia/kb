# Trace news into themes, countries, and companies with Market Catalyst

**Author:** QUICK Corporation  
**Provided:** 2026-08-17  
**Primary asset classes:** Equities, macro, multi-asset

> This page preserves a QUICK-provided use case while removing customer, recipient, signature, and private-link information.

## When to use this workflow

A news headline tells you what happened, but not necessarily how the event connects to your portfolio or what you should investigate next.

Market Catalyst is used here as a bridge from the event itself into **themes, countries, and companies that may be affected**.

The workflow is:

**event → transmission path → next research target**

The goal is not to stop at summarization. It is to turn the news into a structured follow-up queue.

## Example catalyst types in the source

The QUICK material included examples such as:

- an attack on energy infrastructure and the resulting energy-risk premium;
- a US 30-year Treasury auction as a test of long-duration demand;
- revisions to oil-demand forecasts against a backdrop of geopolitical risk;
- US jobless claims as an input into the soft-landing and Fed-policy debate.

These examples span commodities, rates, macro, and equities, which is why the feature works naturally as a multi-asset research entry point.

## Set the relevant region or theme first

The first step is to define the region or theme you care about.

This is not just a display preference. It prevents every headline from receiving equal attention and helps prioritize catalysts that are relevant to the user's coverage universe.

Typical sequence:

1. Open **Insights → Market Catalyst**.
2. Set a theme, region, or both.
3. Compare the displayed catalysts and choose the event that matters most for your mandate.
4. Open the event and review the impact path beyond the headline.
5. Use the linked themes, countries, and companies as candidates for deeper research.

## Why the workflow matters

The useful question after reading a headline is not merely “what happened?” but:

- Which theme is affected?
- Which markets or countries are exposed?
- Which companies are connected to that theme?
- What should I investigate next?

That turns news consumption into a repeatable research process rather than a passive feed.

## How to use the result

Once a catalyst is opened, the downstream entities should be treated as **research candidates**, not automatic investment conclusions.

The next step can be company analysis, theme analysis, or a cross-market comparison depending on the event.
