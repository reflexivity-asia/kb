# Turn today's newsflow into five investment ideas to study

**Author:** Reflexivity Research  
**Primary asset classes:** Multi-asset, macro, equities, fixed income

## Research question

Read the day's news and reduce the flow to five investment ideas that are worth studying further.

Original prompt:

> Read the news and produce the top 5 investment ideas to study based on today's newsflow

## What this use case demonstrates

This workflow is not just a news-summary task. It turns a broad stream of information into a prioritized research queue.

It can be used to:

- extract investable themes from a large volume of news;
- convert news into cross-asset research candidates;
- move from “what happened?” to “what should I investigate next?”;
- use daily news review as the entry point for deeper Reflexivity Research.

## When to use it

This is useful before a morning meeting or market open, when searching for new themes during the day, or when you want to widen the research funnel beyond the asset class you usually cover.

Macro developments, policy changes, company news, commodities, rates, and FX can be scanned together, then reduced to a short list of follow-up questions that deserve deeper work.

The value is in the **prioritization step**: rather than treating every headline as equally important, the workflow produces a manageable set of hypotheses or themes to investigate next.
