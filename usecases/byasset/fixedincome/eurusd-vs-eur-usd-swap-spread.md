# Compare EUR/USD Moves with the EUR-vs-USD Swap Spread

**Author:** Reflexivity Research  
**Primary assets:** Fixed Income, Rates, FX, Multi-Asset

## Research question

Compare the movement in EUR/USD with the movement in the spread between EUR swaps and USD swaps, and examine how changes in FX correspond to changes in relative rates pricing.

Original question:

> Compare moves in EURUSD vs moves in the EUR Swap vs USD Swap spread

## What this use case demonstrates

- bring EUR and USD rates markets into the same analysis as the currency pair rather than looking at FX in isolation;
- compare the EUR-vs-USD swap differential directly with the direction of EUR/USD;
- frame the FX/rates relationship as a cross-asset research question;
- use periods where the two series move together or diverge as the starting point for deeper investigation.

## When to use it

This workflow is useful when an analyst does not want to explain EUR/USD only through central-bank headlines or macro narratives and instead wants to check whether the relative rates market is confirming the FX move.

For a rates investor, it connects changes in swap pricing to the currency market. For a multi-asset investor, it is a compact example of testing the same macro hypothesis across two market expressions.
