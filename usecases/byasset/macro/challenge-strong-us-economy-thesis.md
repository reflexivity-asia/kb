# Challenge a Strong-US-Economy Thesis from the Disconfirming Side

**Author:** Reflexivity Research  
**Primary assets:** Macro, Cross-Asset  
**Intended users:** CIOs, Macro PMs, Multi-Asset PMs, Strategists  
**Analysis type:** Disconfirming analysis, investment-thesis testing, scenario analysis

> This page preserves the underlying Reflexivity research and its dated observations. It is a worked example of testing an investment thesis rather than a current macro forecast.

## Starting question

The research does not try to collect evidence for the headline view that “the US economy is strong.” It asks **where that view is beginning to break down**.

The source conclusion is nuanced: private demand remained strong, but labor momentum, real wages, and the lagged effects of monetary tightening were weakening. It therefore placed the economy closer to a **late-cycle** state than to broad, durable strength.

## Five tests

### 1. Private demand versus headline GDP

- Real final sales to private domestic purchasers: **Q2 2026 +8.5% SAAR**
- Headline real GDP: **+1.48%**

Net exports and inventories depressed headline GDP, while domestic private demand was materially stronger. This is the strongest evidence **against** the rollover thesis.

### 2. Labor market and real wages

- Three-month average NFP: about **71k**
- Source benchmark for employment maintenance: about **150k**
- Real wage growth: **-0.07% YoY**
- Unemployment rate: **4.1%**
- ADP: **+38k**

The source treats this as the clearest area where the strength thesis is fraying.

### 3. Business surveys and PMI

- ISM Manufacturing: **54.6**
- S&P Global Manufacturing PMI: **53.9**
- ISM New Orders: **53.7**
- JOLTS openings: **7.27m**

These readings remain in expansion territory, but the source interprets momentum as slowing rather than accelerating.

### 4. Credit conditions and delinquencies

- C&I tightening: **0.0**, versus 9.5 one year earlier
- Credit-card delinquency rate: **2.85%**, versus 3.22% two years earlier

The source does not treat credit stress as a central recession signal at this stage.

### 5. Rates and the transmission of monetary tightening

- 10Y-2Y: **+0.41 pp**
- 10Y-3M: **+0.87 pp**
- 10Y real yield: **2.42%**
- Fed funds: **3.63%**

The curve inversion had already unwound, but real rates remained restrictive. The source therefore keeps the lagged impact of prior tightening as a continuing headwind.

## Evidence map

| Test | Source signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Private demand | Supports strength | Domestic demand stronger than headline GDP |
| Jobs / real wages | Weakening | Hiring and real wage momentum softer |
| Surveys / PMI | Expansion, but slowing | Above 50, with less momentum |
| Credit | No major stress yet | Lending and delinquency data not showing acute deterioration |
| Rates / policy transmission | Restrictive | Real rates and lagged tightening remain a drag |

## Probability-weighted regime assessment in the source

- **Late cycle: 50%**
- **Durable growth: 30%**
- **Already in slowdown: 20%**

The source's synthesis is that economic strength is real but increasingly narrow and more vulnerable to the effects of restrictive policy.

## Limitations

The source explicitly notes several constraints:

- some series such as ISM and NFIB were not fully available through licensed data in the research run;
- quarterly private-demand estimates can be revised;
- individual regional Fed surveys were not all available.

## How to use the workflow

The useful pattern is to make research act as a **challenge function for the thesis** rather than as a tool for collecting supporting facts.

A reusable sequence is:

**state the thesis → identify the evidence that should falsify it → test several independent channels → preserve the strongest counterevidence → update regime probabilities rather than force a binary answer.**

## What this use case demonstrates

This example shows how a macro thesis can be pressure-tested across demand, labor, surveys, credit, and rates while preserving contradictory evidence instead of smoothing it into a single narrative.
