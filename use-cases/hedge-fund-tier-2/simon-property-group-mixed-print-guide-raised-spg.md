# Simon Property Group mixed print, guide raised (SPG)

**Persona:** Tier 2 Hedge Fund  
**Insight type:** Earnings Catalyst  
**Signal:** Neutral  
**Date:** August 10, 2026

> These examples are dated platform outputs. Check them against current market data before using them, or present them as illustrative examples only.

## Relevant

A Tier 2 PM has to size a large-cap REIT print fast when the headline and the operating metrics disagree.
## Compelling

The platform surfaces the split: Q2 FFO of $3.12 missed the $3.22 consensus, but Real Estate FFO rose 7.9% to $3.29, domestic NOI grew 8.5%, occupancy held at 96.0%, and management raised 2026 FFO guidance to $13.20 to $13.30.
## Workflow

The PM can size on the raise and the capital return (dividend up 4.7% to $2.25, $211.4M of buybacks) while treating the headline FFO miss as an optics issue rather than a demand problem.
