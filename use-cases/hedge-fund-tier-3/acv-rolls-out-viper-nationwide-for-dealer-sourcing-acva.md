## Relevant

This is exactly the kind of under-covered small-cap product catalyst a solo PM has to judge quickly, since the stock fell 14.75% to $6.36 on the day of a nationwide launch.
## Compelling

The platform separates the launch from the numbers: VIPER goes nationwide (installs with no space modifications, scans a vehicle in under 60 seconds), but management reaffirmed FY2026 revenue guidance of $845M to $855M, so the print is an execution-upside option, not a guided growth reset.
## Insight

A PM can run a fast thesis check in minutes and decide whether to fade the sell-off or wait, tracking the real KPIs (dealer-group adoption, unit footprint, and how much service-lane sourcing converts into marketplace volume).
